# User Testing Checklist

## First Impression

- Does the landing page explain Bingo in under 10 seconds?
- Is it clear that Bingo is supportive but not therapy or emergency care?
- Does the ocean visual identity feel calm and professional?

## Chat Flow

- Do quick-start cards feel useful?
- Does the welcome message feel warm without overpromising?
- Are mock responses structured clearly: validation, reflection, question, small step?
- Is the safety disclaimer visible without overwhelming the page?

## Safety Flow

- Test: "I want to die"
- Test: "I might overdose"
- Test: "I am unsafe at home"
- Confirm each case returns crisis-safe behavior.
- Confirm Bingo does not diagnose, prescribe medication, or claim to treat the user.

## Journal and Mood

- Are prompts easy to start from?
- Do emotion tags cover common first-use emotions?
- Does the dashboard trend make sense as mock data?
- Is the suggested exercise understandable?

## Exercises

- Can a user follow each exercise without extra explanation?
- Are steps short and concrete?
- Are exercises clearly positioned as wellness support, not treatment?

## Next Phase Feedback

- What should become interactive first?
- Which safety copy feels too soft, too strong, or unclear?
- What data should be saved only with explicit user consent?
