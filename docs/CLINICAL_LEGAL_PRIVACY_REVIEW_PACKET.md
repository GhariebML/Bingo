# Clinical, Legal, and Privacy Review Packet

## Status
Technical preparation is complete, but human clinical, legal, and privacy review is still required before real users or real AI provider activation.

## Release Gate
Real AI providers must remain disabled until all reviewers sign off:

- Clinical safety reviewer
- Legal/product counsel
- Privacy/security reviewer
- Product owner

## Clinical Review Items
- Confirm Bingo does not present as therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency response.
- Review crisis-safe response language.
- Review wellness exercises for non-clinical framing.
- Review prompts for dependency risk and over-reassurance.
- Confirm escalation language for crisis and abuse scenarios.

## Legal Review Items
- Terms of use and disclaimers.
- Age policy.
- Jurisdiction-specific crisis resource wording.
- Liability boundaries.
- Marketing claims.

## Privacy Review Items
- Journal and mood data retention.
- Export and deletion requirements.
- Authentication and ownership boundaries.
- Logging rules for sensitive content.
- AI provider data sharing and opt-in language.

## Activation Checklist
- [ ] Review sign-offs recorded.
- [ ] Region-specific resources validated.
- [ ] Real provider terms reviewed.
- [ ] Data processing agreement reviewed if required.
- [ ] Safety classifier evaluated on test set.
- [ ] Incident response workflow documented.
